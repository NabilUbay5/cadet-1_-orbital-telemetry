## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-performance aerospace telemetry language rooted in **Digital Brutalism** and **Aero-Minimalism**. Engineered for flight-critical simulation and interactive training, the visual atmosphere rejects consumer fluff, decorative gradients, and soft ambient blur in favor of mathematical precision, architectural discipline, and zero-latency operational feedback.

### Brand Personality & Philosophy
- **Clinical Precision:** Every visual marker corresponds to physical or computational telemetry (acceleration, oxygen reserves, gyroscopic stability, orbital vectors). Nothing exists as mere ornamentation.
- **Mission-Critical Utility:** Interfaces evoke the focused, austere glass cockpits of modern spacecraft and orbital mission control consoles. High-contrast typography and razor-thin architectural boundaries ensure maximum optical legibility under varying cognitive loads.
- **Tactile Brutalism with Aerospace Restraint:** Interactive controls behave like hardware switches and avionics readouts—snappy 1-frame state transitions, high color inversion upon activation, and strict tabular numeric alignment.

### Target Audience
Junior astronauts, simulation pilots, aerospace software operators, and technical cadets who demand rigorous simulation depth combined with modern, high-framerate visual clarity.

## Colors

The color palette is engineered specifically for deep-void dark environments. It leverages photonic accents against ultra-dense carbon and obsidian grounds, preserving night-vision focus and maximizing telemetry contrast.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#00F0FF` - Electric Cyan):** Active telemetry acquisition, primary mission triggers, active target locks, vector trajectories, and focused operational state.
- **Secondary (`#FFB800` - Lunar Amber):** Warning conditions, inertial instability warnings, low oxygen thresholds (< 25%), recalibration indicators, and manual thruster overrides.
- **Tertiary (`#00FF9D` - Orbital Vector Green):** Nominal states, 100% mission readiness, confirmed module docking, and orbital lock verification.
- **Neutral (`#0A0B0E` - Deep Void Obsidian):** Primary viewport backdrop, root WebGL stage background, and deep space canvas.

### Operational Ground & Trench Tiers
- **Surface Panel (`#12141C`):** Base HUD console panels, telemetry deck chassis, and dock containers.
- **Surface Elevated (`#181B26`):** Floating flight cards, active control containers, dialog modals.
- **Surface Trench (`#050608`):** Inset telemetry gutters, recessed gauge slots, and progress track wells.
- **Precision Hairlines (`#2E3446` & `#1C202D`):** Razor-sharp 1px structural grid lines and cell dividing boundaries.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy enforces a dual-engine architecture:
1. **Structural Prose & Heads (Inter):** Geometric grotesque for interface framing, navigation items, system announcements, and instructional manuals.
2. **Avionics & Telemetry (JetBrains Mono):** Tabular, monospaced typography dedicated strictly to physical units, coordinate matrices, operational counters, and system readouts.

### Execution Directives
- **Tabular Numerics:** All real-time telemetry must apply `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` to eliminate layout jitter during high-frequency sensor updates.
- **Unit Coupling:** Dimensional units (`kPa`, `m/s²`, `%`, `BAR`, `G`) sit immediately adjacent to their numeric value at 50% scale, set in `#A0AEC0` or `#64748B`.
- **Labels in Caps:** Avionics labels (`label-caps`) are always rendered in uppercase with positive letter spacing (`+0.08em`) to guarantee quick recognition on dense telemetry wings.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model combines an unbounded viewport stage for 3D WebGL space with a fixed-coordinate HUD perimeter docking system.

### Spatial Distribution
- **Top Command Deck (Height 48px):** Spans 100% viewport width. Houses mission elapsed timer (`MET`), current flight phase status, and mission readiness index.
- **Left Wing Telemetry (Width 280px):** Fixed docking panel reserved for environmental metrics, gravity calibration vectors, and oxygen consumption graphs.
- **Right Wing Telemetry (Width 300px):** Fixed docking panel hosting resource capture inventories, radar azimuths, and agility checklists.
- **Bottom Flight Deck (Height 56px, Max Width 640px):** Floating centered flight instrumentation with key binding indicators and primary actuation triggers.

### Spacing Scale & Rhythm
Spacing adheres to a strict 4px base increment (`space-xs` = 4px, `space-sm` = 8px, `space-md` = 16px, `space-lg` = 24px, `space-xl` = 32px).
Internal component padding never exceeds `16px`–`20px` to maintain mission-grade density. Data rows within telemetry cards maintain a compact `8px` vertical gap.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is established through translucent glass surfaces, razor-sharp 1px boundary rules, and structural contrast rather than heavy diffused drop shadows.

### Atmospheric HUD Glassmorphism
- **Base Surface:** Translucent console charcoal (`rgba(18, 20, 28, 0.82)`).
- **Backdrop Diffusion:** Crisp optical blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`).
- **Surface Inset:** A 1px inner hairline highlight (`inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`) simulating ground-glass edges.

### Structural Boundaries
- **Rule Lines:** High-precision 1px borders using `#2E3446` (resting) or `#00F0FF` (engaged/locked).
- **Elevation Shadow:** A tight, low-diffusion perimeter drop (`0 8px 32px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.75)`) separates floating HUD elements cleanly from active WebGL starfields and planetary bay meshes.

## Shapes

The design system employs **Level 0 (Sharp)** corner geometry. Interfaces conform to brutalist, precision-cut rectangular prisms and sharp chamfered modules.

### Structural Geometry
- **Corners:** 0px radius across panels, button frames, input envelopes, and data cards. Where mechanical softness is needed for ergonomic touch targets, an absolute maximum radius of `2px`–`4px` is permitted on auxiliary controls.
- **Hairline Collisions:** Adjacent data cells share borders via `-1px` negative margins to prevent double-line weight build-up.
- **Segmented Meters:** Gauge indicators are built from discrete mechanical tick blocks rather than continuous rounded pill capsules.

## Components

### 1. Actuators & Buttons
- **Primary Flight Trigger:** Rectangular block (Height 44px), solid Electric Cyan (`#00F0FF`) with Deep Obsidian (`#0A0B0E`) typography in `Inter Bold`. Active state triggers instantaneous inversion: obsidian fill with 1px cyan perimeter and cyan text.
- **Secondary Telemetry Action:** Translucent charcoal fill (`rgba(18, 20, 28, 0.85)`), 1px border (`#2E3446`), Photonic White text. Hover states elevate the border immediately to Electric Cyan.
- **Hazard / Override Button:** Obsidian ground with 1px Lunar Amber (`#FFB800`) border and amber text. On engagement, flips to solid amber with obsidian text.

### 2. HUD Telemetry Cards & Panels
- **Container Structure:** 1px hairline border (`#2E3446`), background `rgba(18, 20, 28, 0.80)`, 16px internal padding.
- **Header Structure:** Includes an uppercase avionics tag (`label-caps`) in `#A0AEC0` paired with an active state beacon (`#00F0FF` or `#00FF9D`).
- **Telemetry Readout:** High-visibility tabular numeric block (`telemetry-lg`) in `#FFFFFF`, with inline baseline-anchored unit symbols in `#64748B`.

### 3. Flight Phase Segmented Selector
- **Architecture:** Segmented brutalist bar anchored across the top display perimeter.
- **Inactive Phase:** `#12141C` fill, `#A0AEC0` text, 11px uppercase monospace index (`01 // GRAVITY CALIBRATION`).
- **Active Phase:** Inset 2px top indicator in Electric Cyan, `#181B26` fill, `#FFFFFF` text, and an illuminated cyan pulse beacon.

### 4. Telemetry Input Fields & Calibrators
- **Numerical Inputs:** Recessed ground (`#0A0B0E`), 1px boundary (`#2E3446`), 8px padding, `JetBrains Mono` text in `#FFFFFF`. Focus transitions border to `#00F0FF` with no outer glow.
- **Stepped Adjustment Sliders:** 4px trench track (`#050608`), filled active segment in `#00F0FF` or `#FFB800`, with a sharp 14×14px square white thumb.

### 5. Checkboxes, Radios & Status Markers
- **Telemetry Checkboxes:** 16×16px sharp square (`0px` radius). Unchecked: `#0A0B0E` well with 1px `#2E3446` edge. Checked: solid `#00F0FF` square containing an obsidian check vector.
- **Status Markers (LED Ticks):** 6×6px solid geometric indicators. Nominal (`#00FF9D`), Standby/Drift (`#FFB800`), Warning/Hazard (`#FF3B30`).

### 6. Domain-Specific Telemetry Modules
- **Resource Matrix:** 3-column micro-grid holding collected telemetry modules, oxygen canisters, and solar grid nodes. Each slot contains a 36×36px recessed trench, wireframe vector icon, and tabular tally (`03/12`).
- **Inertial Stability Reticle:** 2D crosshair with pitch and yaw tick lines, triggering Lunar Amber warning markers when physical drift exceeds `±5°`.
